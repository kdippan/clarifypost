  <p>Nepal has announced its largest-ever national budget, allocating <strong>Rs 2.124 trillion</strong> for Fiscal Year 2083/84 (2026/27). The budget includes major tax relief measures, a 21% salary increase for government employees, infrastructure investment, startup support, AI-focused initiatives, and an ambitious 7% economic growth target.</p>

The Government of Nepal unveiled its annual fiscal budget on May 29, 2026, outlining a roadmap aimed at boosting economic growth, encouraging private-sector investment, creating jobs, and modernizing public services.

The announcement comes as Nepal seeks to accelerate economic recovery, improve revenue collection, attract investment, and address challenges facing businesses and households.

## Nepal Budget 2026 at a Glance

| Category | Allocation |
|-----------|-----------:|
| Total Budget | Rs 2.124 Trillion |
| Current Expenditure | Rs 1.270 Trillion |
| Capital Expenditure | Rs 431 Billion |
| Financial Management | Rs 422 Billion |
| Economic Growth Target | 7% |
| Inflation Target | Below 6% |

The budget is designed to stimulate economic activity while maintaining fiscal stability and supporting long-term development goals.

## Major Tax Relief Announced

One of the most significant aspects of the budget is the introduction of tax relief measures aimed at increasing disposable income and encouraging economic activity.

### Key Tax Changes

- Increased income tax exemption thresholds.
- Reduced tax burden for middle-income earners.
- Simplified tax administration procedures.
- Lower customs duties on selected raw materials.
- Incentives for digital transactions and formal businesses.
- Reforms intended to improve taxpayer compliance.

These measures are expected to provide relief to individuals and businesses while supporting economic expansion.

## Government Employees Receive Salary Increase

The government announced a salary increase of approximately 21% for civil servants and public-sector employees.

Officials believe the increase will help address rising living costs and improve purchasing power across the country. The move is also expected to have a positive impact on domestic consumption and economic activity.

## Focus on Artificial Intelligence and Technology

For the first time, Nepal's national budget places a strong emphasis on artificial intelligence, digital innovation, and technology-driven growth.

### Technology Initiatives

- Establishment of AI and digital innovation programs.
- Support for Nepal's growing startup ecosystem.
- Incentives for IT exports and digital businesses.
- Expansion of e-governance services.
- Increased investment in research and development.
- Promotion of technology-based entrepreneurship.

The government aims to position Nepal as a competitive destination for digital innovation and technology investment in South Asia.

## Infrastructure Remains a Priority

Infrastructure development continues to receive significant attention in the new budget.

### Key Areas of Investment

- National highways and road networks.
- Hydropower and energy projects.
- Irrigation systems and agricultural infrastructure.
- Airport upgrades and transportation improvements.
- Urban development initiatives.
- Digital infrastructure expansion.

Improving infrastructure is viewed as essential for increasing productivity, attracting investment, and supporting long-term economic growth.

## Agriculture and Rural Development

Agriculture remains a major contributor to Nepal's economy, and the budget includes measures designed to improve productivity and support farmers.

### Agricultural Priorities

- Expansion of irrigation facilities.
- Agricultural modernization programs.
- Support for commercial farming.
- Access to agricultural credit.
- Increased investment in rural development projects.

Officials hope these measures will reduce import dependency and strengthen domestic agricultural production.

## Education and Healthcare Spending

The government has also allocated significant resources to improve education and healthcare services.

### Education

- Increased funding for public schools.
- Expansion of scholarship programs.
- Technical and vocational training initiatives.
- Investment in higher education infrastructure.

### Healthcare

- Strengthening public health facilities.
- Increased healthcare infrastructure spending.
- Improved access to medical services in rural areas.
- Expansion of health insurance programs.

These investments are intended to improve human capital development and enhance quality of life.

## Can Nepal Achieve 7% Economic Growth?

The government has set a target of 7% economic growth while aiming to keep inflation below 6%.

Achieving this goal will depend on several factors:

- Strong private-sector investment.
- Effective budget implementation.
- Growth in tourism and exports.
- Continued remittance inflows.
- Faster infrastructure project execution.
- Political and economic stability.

While analysts consider the target ambitious, many agree that successful implementation of the announced reforms could improve investor confidence and economic performance.
