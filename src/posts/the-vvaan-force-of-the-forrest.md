# What Is The Vvaan: Force of the Forrest About?

At the centre of The Vvaan is a man who believes in logic.

He comes from an urban, modern world and does not easily accept the supernatural beliefs of his ancestral village.

When he returns to his family's roots, he encounters warnings about a forest that the villagers consider forbidden and sacred.

He initially treats those warnings as superstition.

Then he crosses the boundary.

That decision changes everything.

According to the published synopsis, entering the forbidden forest awakens an ancient divine force described as goddess-like. The protagonist is then forced to confront events that cannot be explained through the rational worldview he previously trusted.

What begins as disbelief develops into a much larger struggle involving:

- Ancestral beliefs
- Indian folklore
- A mysterious forest
- A supernatural presence
- Faith versus rationalism
- The survival of a community
- A reluctant transformation into a protector

The story is deliberately keeping many details hidden, which makes the central mystery one of the film's biggest attractions.

## The Central Idea: Logic vs Faith

One of the most interesting things about The Vvaan is not simply the presence of supernatural forces.

It is the conflict between two worldviews.

On one side is modernity.

The protagonist approaches the world through logic and rational explanation.

On the other side is a village culture shaped by generations of stories, traditions, warnings, and beliefs connected to the forest.

The film places those two worlds directly against each other.

That gives The Vvaan a potentially richer foundation than a conventional creature or haunted-forest story.

The question is not merely:

<strong>"What is inside the forest?"</strong>

It is also:

<strong>"What happens when a person who does not believe in the supernatural becomes impossible to separate from it?"</strong>

# Why Is The Vvaan Trending?

The movie has been attracting attention for several reasons.

First, it reunites a major Hindi-film lead pair in a genre that is relatively unusual for mainstream Bollywood.

Second, the story is rooted in Indian folklore instead of relying entirely on an established international fantasy property.

Third, the filmmakers are presenting The Vvaan as a world with its own mythology.

And finally, the official teaser released on August 25, 2026 gave audiences their first substantial visual look at the film.

The teaser immediately raised questions around the forest, the divine presence, Sidharth Malhotra's character, and the larger mythology behind the title.

The official promotional material also confirms the September 25 theatrical release.

## Sidharth Malhotra as the Central Character

Sidharth Malhotra leads the film as the rational urban man at the heart of the story.

That character setup gives him a different kind of challenge from a straightforward action hero.

He begins as someone who trusts reason more than folklore.

But The Vvaan appears designed to gradually strip away that certainty.

The forest becomes more than a location.

It becomes the force that challenges everything he thinks he knows.

The central character therefore has an emotional journey as well as a physical one:

```text
Modern city life
      ↓
Return to ancestral roots
      ↓
Dismissal of local beliefs
      ↓
Entry into the forbidden forest
      ↓
Encounter with the supernatural
      ↓
Crisis
      ↓
Transformation
      ↓
Protector of his people
````

That transformation is one of the most important elements to watch when the film arrives.

## Tamannaah Bhatia's Role

Tamannaah Bhatia joins Sidharth Malhotra as the female lead.

That leaves plenty of room for the theatrical film to reveal how her character fits into the mythology.

# Who Directed The Vvaan?

The film is associated with **Arunabh Kumar** and **Deepak Kumar Mishra**.

Arunabh Kumar is one of the founders and key creative figures behind The Viral Fever, while Deepak Kumar Mishra is known for his work in TVF productions.

The combination is particularly notable.

TVF built its reputation around character-driven stories, grounded settings, relatable humour, and strong writing.

The Vvaan moves into a much larger cinematic space involving folklore, fantasy, supernatural mystery, action, and visual world-building.

That makes the film an interesting creative expansion for the TVF ecosystem.

# Who Wrote The Vvaan?

The screenplay and story are credited to **Arunabh Kumar**, while dialogue is credited to **Anandeshwar Dwivedi** in the current promotional credits.

The story appears to balance two worlds:

<strong>modern urban rationalism</strong>

and

<strong>traditional folklore and supernatural belief.</strong>

That thematic contrast is likely to drive much of the movie rather than simply serving as background decoration.

# Who Produced The Vvaan?

The Vvaan is being presented by **Balaji Motion Pictures**, in association with **TVF Motion Pictures** and **11:11 Productions**.

The current producer credits include:

* Shobha Kapoor
* Ektaa R Kapoor
* Arunabh Kumar
* Niraj Kothari

This collaboration is one of the film's most interesting industry angles.

Balaji brings decades of mainstream Hindi entertainment experience, while TVF brings a modern digital storytelling identity and a strong history of character-led Indian narratives.

The Vvaan therefore sits at an unusual intersection between mainstream Bollywood spectacle and TVF's storytelling sensibilities.

# The World of The Vvaan

A major selling point of the film is its world.

The story is not set against a generic fantasy landscape.

It is rooted in an Indian village, an ancestral connection, a forbidden forest, and folklore surrounding a powerful divine presence.

That gives the film an opportunity to build mythology from Indian cultural references rather than importing a fantasy vocabulary from Western cinema.

The forest appears to function almost like a character itself.

It represents:

* Memory
* Fear
* Faith
* Nature
* Ancestry
* The unknown
* The supernatural

And that creates a strong thematic question:

<strong>Can humanity control a force it does not understand?</strong>

# The Folklore Behind The Story

The film's premise draws from the long tradition of Indian folklore in which forests, village boundaries, sacred spaces, local deities, spirits, and ancestral stories are closely connected.

The Vvaan is not presented as a direct adaptation of one famous mythological story.

Instead, its premise appears to create an original fictional mythology inspired by Indian folklore.

That distinction is important.

The film is attempting to build a new cinematic world while using familiar cultural ideas about sacred forests, divine forces, and the relationship between humans and nature.

# The Vvaan Is More Than a Horror Movie

Some entertainment listings have described the film as horror or horror-thriller.

IMDb currently classifies it across:

* Action

* Mystery
* Thriller

Other descriptions emphasize mythology, folklore, adventure, and supernatural elements.

The safest description is therefore:

<strong>a folklore-driven fantasy thriller with supernatural and horror elements.</strong>

That combination could make the movie feel very different from a conventional Bollywood horror film.

The focus appears to be on mythology, mystery, adventure, action, and world-building rather than horror alone.

# A Major Production Decision: The Additional Shoot

The film reportedly underwent an additional **10-day shoot** focused particularly on the climax and several important sequences.

According to reports, the creative team reviewed the final cut and concluded that certain scenes could be made larger, more dramatic, and more impactful for theatrical audiences.

Instead of simply accepting the existing cut, the filmmakers chose to revisit selected portions of the movie.

That suggests the climax is expected to be an important part of the theatrical experience.

For a film built around supernatural mystery, the final act can determine whether the mythology feels satisfying or underwhelming.

The additional shooting therefore becomes a noteworthy production detail.

That distinction matters because viewers may encounter familiar cultural symbols without the movie being a literal adaptation of a traditional scripture or historical legend.

# Why The Vvaan Could Be Important for Indian Cinema

Indian cinema has a long history of stories involving folklore, mythology, local deities, spirits, sacred landscapes, and supernatural traditions.

But many such stories have traditionally remained associated with regional cinema.
